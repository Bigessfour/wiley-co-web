# Clerk quick start

One page for Town Hub. Call Town Hall at (719) 829-4974 or email clerk@townofwiley.gov if a step fails. Town Hall is at 304 Main Street. Do not email your password.

## Sign in

1. Open Town Hub (Wiley Widget).
2. Choose **Sign in**. The town login page opens. It is the same login Trash Truck uses.
3. Come back to Town Hub. Your name shows in the shell. **Sign out** signs you out of Trash Truck too, once the town login domain is in production.

If sign-in stops, the page tells you to call Town Hall. You can also open **Help** (`/support`).

## Cards

| Card | What it is | Sign-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wiley Widget | Water rate study in this app | Town Hub sign-in |
| Trash Truck | Routes | Same Town Hub sign-in |
| Apartments | Apartment operations | Office network only. Sign in again there. |
| TIKR | TIKR | Office network only. Sign in again there. |
| Rural Water | Not open yet | Coming soon |

Drivers see Trash Truck. Clerks and admins see the rest. Apartments and TIKR need the office network (Tailscale or the town LAN). They are not on the public internet.
